The Equilune Calendar

Two ideas. One question.

What if we could organize time differently?
The Equilune Calendar explores two connected ideas:


1: A simpler calendar.

Twelve equal months, predictable weekdays,
and a structure designed to be reused.



2: A new way to organize holidays.

Theme Weeks that make room for remembrance,
celebration, culture, and history yet to be written.



Together, they invite us to reconsider something
we have inherited for generations:

Does the way we organize time still serve
the way we live?
